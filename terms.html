Terms of use

Effective 10 October 2026

Iceland Trail Demand is operated by Ari Arnalds for personal and internal keyword-demand research. This website explains the application; it does not provide a public dashboard, visitor accounts, or a paid service.

Purpose and authorized use

The tracker collects and compares aggregate Google Ads Keyword Planner estimates related to Iceland hiking and trail-running. Use requires the account owner’s authorization, appropriate Google Ads account access, and compliance with Google’s applicable API terms and policies. Do not use another person’s credentials or attempt unauthorized access.

Research estimates

Keyword Planner statistics are estimates and may be rounded, combined across related searches, revised, or unavailable. They are indicators of search interest, not promises of bookings, revenue, campaign performance, or individual traveler behavior. They should be considered alongside other research.

Availability and account control

The tracker depends on Google’s services, API availability, quotas, and valid credentials. Collection may be interrupted or require changes when those services change. The owner can stop collection, remove credentials, or revoke Google authorization at any time.

Data handling

The privacy policy explains the information accessed, how it is used and retained, and how to revoke access or delete stored material. Credentials and private reports must not be published in the public information website.

Relationship to Google and GitHub

Iceland Trail Demand is an independent research tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Google or GitHub. Their respective services remain subject to their own terms and policies.

Changes and contact

Changes to these terms will be posted here with an updated effective date. For questions, contact Ari Arnalds at ariparnalds@gmail.com.
